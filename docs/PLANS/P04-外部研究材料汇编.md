## 一、渐进式披露（Progressive Disclosure）—— 解决「内容太多、没有层层递进」

**老罗原话**：「内容太多了，都没有层层递进」「不能溢出很多」

### 1.1 核心定义与三层结构

**来源**：Stellae Design · Progressive Content Disclosure
`https://www.stellae.design/en/ux/progressive-content-disclosure`

> Progressive content disclosure is the strategy of layering information from simple to complex. Users see a summary first and can drill into details on demand. This reduces initial cognitive load while making comprehensive information available for those who need it.

三层模型：
1. **Surface**：默认可见 —— 标题、摘要、主 CTA
2. **First click**：按需展开 —— 可折叠区、标签页、弹窗
3. **Deep dive**：深度探索 —— 完整文档、进阶设置

**本轮落地**：`/toolbox/library.html` 的 L1–L5 五层就是这套模型的实例化。

### 1.2 关键警告：不要把重要信息藏起来

**来源**：同上

> The most common mistake is hiding essential information behind disclosure interactions under the guise of progressive disclosure — if 80% of users need a piece of information to complete their primary task, that information belongs in the first visible layer regardless of how clean the interface looks without it, because progressive disclosure is about managing complexity, not hiding content to achieve visual minimalism.

**本轮落地**：工具库的**结论（4 条带出处）放最上层**，没有藏进折叠区；藏起来的只有 `deepdive`（核心用法与独门技巧）。

### 1.3 不要制造多层点击链

**来源**：同上

> Another frequent error is creating too many disclosure levels with too little content at each, forcing users through a chain of clicks to reach information that could have been presented in two well-organized layers — three clicks to reach a two-sentence answer is not progressive disclosure, it is poor information architecture.

**本轮落地**：工具库深挖只有**一层** `<details>`，没有二级三级套娃。

### 1.4 展开后要保持上下文

**来源**：同上

> Teams also neglect to maintain context across disclosure levels: when a user expands a detail panel, the summary that motivated the expansion should remain visible so the user can reference it while reading the details, and collapsing one section should not scroll the user away from their current position on the page.

**本轮落地**：`<details>` 在卡片内部，卡片本身的用途/关键词/价格始终可见，展开不遮挡上下文。

### 1.5 五步设计流程

**来源**：UXPin · What Is Progressive Disclosure in UX
`https://www.uxpin.com/studio?p=41489`

> Step 1: Identify User Needs → Step 2: Prioritize Information → Step 3: Determine the Right Level of Detail → Step 4: Design for Simplicity → Step 5: Prototype and Test

John Maeda《10 Laws of Simplicity》里与本轮最相关的两条：
- **Reduce**：删掉不需要的（本轮：首页 6 个 chip 减到 1 主 3 次）
- **Organize**：把复杂系统变简单（本轮：37,365 字符单页 → 5 层递进）

### 1.6 信息架构层面的原则

**来源**：Microsoft Learn · 实现信息体系结构的建议
`https://learn.microsoft.com/zh-cn/power-platform/well-architected/experience-optimization/information-architecture`

> 将信息和功能组织成有意义的逻辑组，会让用户更容易查找和访问工作负荷的信息和功能。
> 根据使用的重要性、频率或顺序，按层次顺序排列和显示内容和功能。
> 使用可视提示和指示器（如大小、颜色、形状或位置）显示层次关系和级别。

**本轮落地**：h1 → h2 → h3 → 卡片标题四级层次；分类用图标砖 + 颜色区分；工具卡用品牌标砖。

### 1.7 决策路径设计

**来源**：Yber Digital · Progressive Disclosure: Designing Complex Service Offerings Into Simple Decisions
`https://www.yberdigitals.com/post/progressive-disclosure-designing-complex-service-offerings-into-simple-decisions`

> The objective is not to hide available services. It is to present them in an order that matches how people make decisions.

其中「我要做一件事」这类引导式路径的做法：

> A short decision tool can ask a series of questions and recommend the most relevant service category.

**本轮落地**：L3「我要做一件事，该用哪几个」正是这种决策路径 —— 按场景给配方，不按厂商排。

**另一条硬约束**：

> Do Not Make Visitors Hunt for Important Information — Some information should remain immediately accessible. That includes: What the business does · Who the service is for · The primary value proposition · The main conversion path

**本轮落地**：首页首屏必须有「一句话定位 + 1 个主 CTA」，不能一进来就让人猜。

## 二、来源标注与可信度 —— 解决「没有多少有出处的」

**老罗原话**：「也没有多少有出处的」「我希望……而且要有来源」

### 2.1 来源可信度的六项判据

**来源**：Cite This For Me · Reference a Website
`https://www.citethisforme.com/cite/website`

> - **Purpose**：这个来源存在的目的是什么？信息还是观点？作者是否公开说明意图？
> - **Authority — Author**：谁是作者？有什么资历？有没有明确联系方式？
> - **Authority — Publisher**：出版方是谁？非营利组织/政府机构/商业网站，立场可能不同
> - **Accuracy**：信息能否被验证？是否有明确引用的证据支持？
> - **Relevance**：与你的话题相关吗？受众匹配吗？
> - **Currency**：发布时间是什么时候？最后何时更新？反映的是当前可得的最新信息吗？

**本轮落地**：工具库每个工具卡同时给 **官网链接（Authority/Accuracy）+ 核验日期 checkedAt（Currency）**；
每条结论给 **出处名 + 原文链接（Authority + Accuracy）**。

### 2.2 引用必须真的存在、且真的支持该说法

**来源**：Bond University Library · How can I tell if a citation really exists or is accurate?
`http://bond.libanswers.com/faq/273165`

> AI tools, search engines, and online platforms can sometimes provide citations that appear accurate but do not exist, or to real sources that do not contain the information presented. Verifying a source means going back to the original work and checking that the information, quotation, statistic, or idea has been reported accurately and in the correct context.

要特别小心这些信号：
> Be cautious of citations that: Cannot be found in reputable databases · Contain incomplete, inconsistent, or inaccurate information · Have authors who cannot be verified · Were generated by AI tools without a traceable or verifiable source

**本轮落地**：这直接支撑了「未直连核实的工具不标『不可用』，而标具体原因」的处理 ——
429/403/000 都是**本机出口**问题，不是站点不可用，如实标注原因比笼统说「未核实」更严谨。

### 2.3 识别「装饰性引用」与「断链引用」

**来源**：AIMR Publications · How to read online citations so you can trust what you are actually reading
`https://aimrpubs.org/how-to-read-online-citations-so-you-can-trust-what-you-are-actually-reading-1500`

> Yet a surprising number of those citations do not say what the writer suggests, are out of date, or lead to low quality material.

三种典型注水手法：
> - **Endless reference chains**：A cites B, B cites C, C cites a dead link
> - **Vague citations**：「scientists say」「experts agree」，不具名
> - **Broken or irrelevant links**：链接指向首页、被墙文件或无关文章

> Click through and compare claim and source — Whenever a claim seems surprising, unusually convenient, or emotionally charged, follow the link that is supposed to support it.

**本轮落地**：工具库的「本页所有出处」章节把 42 条链接集中列出，便于逐条核对；
工具卡的「打开官网」指向的是官网本身，不是首页或搜索页。

### 2.4 内容出处溯源（provenance）的行业实践

**来源**：BBC R&D · Did they really say that? Proving the source behind a claim in the age of AI
`https://www.bbc.com/rd/articles/2026-06-verify-news-quotes-claims-provenance-source`

> With trust under pressure from AI-generated content and misinformation, a proof of concept shows how claims can be linked to verified sources, so readers can check what was said and who said it.

核心机制：把主张绑定到**可验证的源快照**（源被删改时，绑定失效并告警）。

> The aim is to publish news articles which attribute facts to canonical sources.

**本轮落地**：`data/ai-tools.json` 的 `checkedAt` + `verified` 字段就是这个思路的轻量实现 ——
每条数据带核验时间，数据更新时可对比差异。

## 三、命名与定位一致性 —— 解决「有 7 个说法」

**老罗原话**：「每次规划都做成文档」「别人问我这是什么东西，我根本就答不上来」

（相关的命名规范见 P02 材料汇编，本轮不再重复。）

本轮新增的关键做法：**唯一真源 + 生成器注入**。
`data/positioning.json` 是唯一定位来源，`scripts/gen-positioning.py` 负责注入全站；
改一处即改全站，不可能再出现 7 个说法。
